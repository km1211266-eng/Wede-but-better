About us

Why we're called Kwacha

The name

Kwacha is a Bemba and Nyanja word meaning "it is dawn" — it's also the name of the currency in Zambia and Malawi, two of the countries our green coffee comes from. We liked that it pointed in both directions at once: first light, and where the beans are grown.

How we started

The roastery began in 2024 in a spare room in Brixton, with a secondhand sample roaster that could barely handle a kilogram at a time and a lot of burnt batches nobody else had to drink. Two years later we roast on a proper drum machine, supply a handful of cafés around Johannesburg, and still throw out anything that doesn't cup well — including our own mistakes.

What we're trying to do

Roast honestly, date every bag, and pay farms enough that they're still there next harvest. We'd rather sell out of a small lot than water down what made it good in the first place.
