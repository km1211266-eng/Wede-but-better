Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer delivery?

Yes — we deliver within Johannesburg. Enquire through the contact page for current delivery timing and cost.

How fresh is the coffee?

Every bag is roasted within seven days of dispatch, and the roast date is printed on the bag — not a vague "best before."

Can I buy wholesale?

Yes, we already supply a small number of local cafés. Contact us for wholesale pricing and minimum order quantities.

Whole bean or ground?

Both. Tell us your brew method when you order and we'll grind to match it, right before dispatch.

Where does the coffee come from?

Current lots come from Mzuzu in Malawi, the Copperbelt in Zambia, and Magoebaskloof here in Limpopo. See Our Roasts for what's on right now.
